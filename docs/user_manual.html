1. Introduction

Welcome to Pectus AI — the first AI-powered mobile application dedicated entirely to Pectus Excavatum recovery. This manual will guide you through everything you need to know to get the most out of the app.

Pectus Excavatum is a chest wall deformity where the breastbone is sunken into the chest. While surgical correction is sometimes needed, daily targeted exercises can significantly improve posture, breathing capacity, and quality of life — especially for mild to moderate cases. Pectus AI provides exactly those tools.

2. Installation Guide

Installing via APK

1
Download the APKDownload PectusAI.apk from the provided link or the apk/ folder in this repository.
2
Enable Unknown SourcesGo to Android Settings → Security (or Privacy) → Enable "Unknown Sources" or "Install Unknown Apps".
3
Open the APKTap the downloaded file in your notification bar or file manager to begin installation.
4
Install & LaunchTap Install, wait for completion, then open Pectus AI from your app drawer.
⚠️ Tip: If you are on Android 8.0 or above, you may need to allow the specific app (e.g., Chrome or your file manager) to install unknown apps. Go to Settings → Apps → Special Access → Install Unknown Apps.

5. Using the AI Analysis

AI Chat Assistant

Tap the AI Chat button (the glowing bubble in the navigation bar) to open the chat interface. You can ask anything related to Pectus Excavatum — exercises, severity, diet, or general recovery questions.

Chest Photo Analysis

From the Dashboard, tap the "AI Chest Analysis" card. You can:

  • Upload a chest photo from your gallery, or take one with your camera.
  • The AI will analyze the image and provide a severity estimate.
  • You will receive a customized exercise plan based on the analysis.
🔒 Privacy Note: Your photos are sent to the AI provider for analysis only. They are not stored by this app. For maximum privacy, use Ollama Local Mode (see Settings).

6. Settings & Customization

Access all settings via the Profile tab (bottom navigation).

  • Edit Name: Tap the pencil icon next to your name to change it.
  • Dark / Light Mode: Toggle the theme switch to change the app's appearance.
  • Notification Reminders: Enable daily reminders and set your preferred workout time.
  • AI Provider: Switch between Groq (cloud) and Ollama (local) for AI features.
  • Clear Progress: Reset your session data from the Profile screen if needed.

7. FAQ & Troubleshooting

The AI chat is not responding

Make sure you have a working internet connection if using Groq or Gemini mode. If using Ollama, ensure the Ollama server is running on your local machine.

The app crashes on startup

Try clearing the app's cache: Settings → Apps → Pectus AI → Clear Cache, then relaunch.

Notifications are not appearing

Go to Settings → Apps → Pectus AI → Notifications and ensure notifications are enabled for this app.

Camera permission denied

Go to Settings → Apps → Pectus AI → Permissions and enable Camera and Storage permissions.
